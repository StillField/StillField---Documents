Project Statement

Architectural Context

The Assembly Hall forms part of the historic Rajghat Besant School campus in Varanasi. The hall is a significant architectural and institutional space and the audio intervention is therefore approached as a reversible, carefully coordinated addition rather than a conventional installation.

Design Development

The system will be developed from measured room behaviour, programme requirements and subsequent system verification. Detailed loudspeaker positions, orientation, zoning, DSP architecture, signal routing, delay structure and final processing parameters are established through the engineering process rather than fixed at preliminary stage.

03

Design Principles

  • Speech intelligibility is the primary performance criterion.
  • The system should support assemblies, speeches, cultural programmes and light music without being treated as a high-output concert system.
  • Intervention should respect the historic building and avoid wall drilling.
  • Freestanding, weighted speaker supports are preferred wherever practical.
  • System architecture, zoning, timing and processing should be measurement-led.
  • Equipment selection should balance professional reliability with responsible school expenditure.
04

Engineering Method

Detailed system parameters will be established following on-site acoustic measurements and engineering evaluation. The measurement programme forms the engineering basis for subsequent loudspeaker selection, placement, aiming, system zoning, DSP architecture and commissioning parameters.

  1. Ambient Noise Survey
    Establishes the background-noise environment and reference conditions for speech and programme levels.
  2. Frequency Response / Room Response
    Evaluates spectral behaviour and persistent frequency-dependent characteristics of the hall.
  3. Impulse Response & Energy Time Curve (ETC)
    Identifies direct arrival and significant reflections and relates them to the room geometry.
  4. Reverberation & Decay Analysis
    Evaluates EDT, T20/T30 and frequency-dependent decay behaviour.
  5. Speech & Music Clarity Analysis
    Evaluates C50, C80 and D50 as indicators of programme clarity.
  6. Spatial Uniformity Survey
    Measures variation across representative listener positions.
  7. Low-Frequency Decay & Modal Behaviour
    Identifies resonances and decay characteristics relevant to programme and system design.

Design IP & Disclosure

The detailed electroacoustic solution constitutes professional design work. Final coordinates, speaker topology, DSP architecture, timing parameters, processing values and equipment schedule form part of the subsequent engineering package.

These parameters are not fixed at the preliminary stage because they are derived from measured acoustic behaviour, programme requirements and subsequent system verification. This document establishes the method and commercial framework; it is not a substitute for the final engineering package.
